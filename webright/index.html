06/ Process

A clear process.
Shaped around the project.

This is the general framework. Not every project uses every stage in the same way — a copywriting project isn't forced through a software-development process.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business, audience, problem, goals and existing presence.

  2. 02

    Define

    Determine requirements, scope, deliverables, specialists, timeline and budget.

  3. 03

    Create

    Design, write, strategise or produce what the project requires.

  4. 04

    Build

    Where development is involved

    Turn approved ideas into working digital experiences.

  5. 05

    Test & Refine

    Review the work, test what needs testing and refine within the agreed scope.

  6. 06

    Deliver / Launch

    Publish, launch or hand over the completed work.

  7. 07

    Support

    Where agreed

    Provide agreed post-project support and ongoing services where required.
